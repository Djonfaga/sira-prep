# Privacy Policy — TOEFLsix

TOEFLsix ("the app") is an independent English-exam study tool. This policy explains
what data the app handles. **Fill the bracketed fields and host this page at a public
URL, then add that URL in Google Play Console → App content → Privacy policy.**

## Summary
The app has **no accounts and no servers of ours**. Your study progress and settings
stay **on your device**. The app shows **no ads** and uses **no analytics or tracking
SDKs**. Two features send data off the device, and only when you use them, as described
below.

## What the app stores on your device
- Study/mock progress, selected exam, language, and appearance settings.
- If you enable the optional AI coach, the **AI provider you choose, the model name, and
  the API key you enter** — stored locally on your device only.

These are kept in the device's local app storage and are removed if you clear the app's
data or uninstall it. "Reset progress" in Settings clears your saved practice for the
selected exam.

## Data sent off the device (only when you use these features)
1. **AI coach ("Sira") — optional, off by default.** If you configure it, the messages
   you send are transmitted over HTTPS to the **third-party AI provider you select**
   (e.g. OpenRouter, Groq, or Cerebras) using the API key you provide. That data is
   handled under **that provider's** privacy policy, not ours. We do not receive or store
   your conversations. Do not enter sensitive personal information in the coach.
2. **Speaking/voice practice — optional.** When you use voice features, the app uses your
   device's built-in speech-recognition service to convert speech to text. On most Android
   devices this is provided by the operating system / Google and is subject to Google's
   privacy terms. The app does not record, store, or upload audio itself.

## Permissions
- **Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO):** used only for the speaking/voice practice features above.
- **Internet:** used to reach the AI provider (if enabled) and load content.

## What we do NOT do
- No advertising, no analytics, no third-party tracking or profiling.
- No selling or sharing of personal data for advertising.
- No user accounts, sign-in, or server-side profiles operated by us.

## Children
The app is intended for general audiences preparing for English proficiency exams and is
not directed at children under 13.
